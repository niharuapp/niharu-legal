# Privacy Policy — Nanoka for Android

*An app by Niharu* · Last updated: October 4, 2026

🇦🇷 [Leer esta política en español](politica-de-privacidad.md)

## 1. Scope of this policy

This policy applies **only to Nanoka for Android** (七日, "seven days"), a weekly planner for organizing your week and building habits, with an optional home screen widget.

Nanoka **does not use a NiHaru account** or any login. If you use the website [niharuapp.com](https://niharuapp.com) or other Niharu apps, each one is governed by its own privacy policy.

## 2. Who is responsible

- **Developer and owner:** Santiago Agustín Playa, developer and owner of Niharu, Argentina.
- **Legal address:** Autonomous City of Buenos Aires, Argentina. The full address is available upon request of the supervisory authority.
- **Contact:** hola@niharuapp.com

## 3. Data the app handles

| Data | Purpose | Where it lives |
|------|---------|----------------|
| **Weeks (templates), blocks, and categories** that you create | Show and organize your weekly plan | Your device only |
| **App settings** (active week, alert preferences, visual settings) | Make the app and the widget look and behave as you configured them | Your device only |
| **Scheduled alerts** for the start of each block, if you turn them on | Notify you when a block starts | Your device only |

All of this information is stored in the app's internal storage on your device. **It is never sent to us or to any server**, and we have no way to see or access it.

## 4. Data we do NOT collect

- Name, email, password, or any account data: Nanoka has no accounts or sign-ups.
- Location, contacts, device accounts, advertising ID, or device identifiers.
- Analytics or telemetry: the app has no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and your taps and usage are not logged.
- Any information sent to third parties: we do not use advertising or third-party trackers.

The app does not need an internet connection to work.

## 5. Permissions the app uses

| Permission | Why |
|------------|-----|
| **Notifications** | Alert you when a block starts, if you turn that option on |
| **Exact alarms and reminders** | Make those alerts arrive at the time you set |
| **Device startup** | Reschedule your alerts after your phone restarts |

We use these permissions only for the purposes above. The widget shows your week on your home screen and does not send information anywhere.

## 6. How we use the data

Only to show and organize your weekly plan on your own device. **We do not collect, sell, or share your data, run advertising, or build profiles.**

## 7. Where data is stored and who processes it

Your data is stored only on your device. There is no server, database, or third-party processor involved in the operation of the app.

## 8. How long we keep data and how to delete it

Your data stays on your device while you use the app. You can delete weeks, blocks, and categories from within the app at any time. If you uninstall Nanoka, all the data it stored on your device is deleted.

## 9. Android backups

If Android backup is enabled on your Google account, the system may include Nanoka's data in that backup. This is managed by Google under its own policies, not this one; the app does not send anything on its own.

## 10. Your rights

Under Argentina's Personal Data Protection Law No. 25,326, you have the right to **access, correct, and delete** your personal data. Since Nanoka does not collect or receive your data, we hold no information about you that we could provide, correct, or delete; everything is under your control on your device.

If you have any questions about this, you can write to **hola@niharuapp.com**.

Argentina's **Agency for Access to Public Information (AAIP)**, as the supervisory authority of Law No. 25,326, is empowered to handle complaints from anyone whose rights are affected by non-compliance with the personal data protection rules in force.

## 11. Children

Nanoka is not directed at children under 13. Since the app does not collect data from anyone, we do not store information about children.
